# NutriBox: KPI Definitions & Measurement Framework
**Project:** Founder's Office Dashboard  
**Business Model:** Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) Healthy Snack Subscription  
**Geography & Currency:** India (INR, ₹)  
**Reporting Horizon:** 52 Weeks (12 Months Trailing)

---

## Executive Overview
This document provides the canonical definition, mathematical formulation, underlying data lineage, and operational interpretation for all Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) monitored in the Founder's Office Dashboard.

---

## 1. Sales & Revenue KPIs

### 1.1 Delivered Revenue (Weekly & Monthly)
* **Definition:** Total gross recognized value of orders successfully delivered to customers during the reporting period. Orders still pending fulfillment or cancelled/refunded are excluded from recognized top-line revenue.
* **Formula:**  
  $$\text{Delivered Revenue} = \sum (\text{Quantity} \times \text{Unit Price}) \quad \forall \; \text{orders with } \text{status} = \text{'Delivered'}$$
* **Data Lineage:** `orders.csv` (`revenue` where `status = 'Delivered'`), reconciled against `finance.csv` (`revenue`).
* **Format:** Currency (`₹#,##0`).

### 1.2 Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
* **Definition:** The predictable monthly subscription revenue generated by all actively subscribed customers at the end of the evaluation week.
* **Formula:**  
  $$\text{MRR} = \sum_{i \in \text{Active Customers}} \text{Monthly Plan Price}_i$$
* **Data Lineage:** `subscriptions.csv` (`monthly_price`), filtered where customer is active (`signup_date <= Week End` and `churn_date is NULL or > Week End`).
* **Format:** Currency (`₹#,##0`).

### 1.3 Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)
* **Definition:** The annualized run-rate of the current subscription subscriber base.
* **Formula:**  
  $$\text{ARR} = \text{MRR} \times 12$$
* **Format:** Currency (`₹#,##0`).

### 1.4 Orders Placed
* **Definition:** Total count of unique subscription boxes and add-on orders generated during the week across all fulfillment statuses.
* **Formula:**  
  $$\text{Orders} = \text{COUNT}(\text{order\_id})$$
* **Data Lineage:** `orders.csv` (`order_id`).
* **Format:** Integer (`#,##0`).

### 1.5 Active Customer Base
* **Definition:** Total cumulative unique customers who have signed up and have not churned as of the close of the reporting week.
* **Formula:**  
  $$\text{Active Customers} = \text{COUNT}(\text{customer\_id}) \quad \text{where } \text{signup\_date} \le T_{\text{end}} \text{ and } (\text{churn\_date} > T_{\text{end}} \lor \text{churn\_date is NULL})$$
* **Data Lineage:** `customers.csv`.
* **Format:** Integer (`#,##0`).

## 2. Marketing & Acquisition KPIs

### 2.1 Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
* **Definition:** The blended or channel-specific marketing cost required to acquire one newly subscribed paying customer.
* **Formula:**  
  $$\text{CAC} = \frac{\text{Total Marketing Spend in Period}}{\text{New Customer Signups in Period}}$$
* **Data Lineage:** `marketing.csv` (`spend`), `customers.csv` (`signup_date`).
* **Target / Benchmark:** $< ₹8,500$ blended; ideally $< \frac{1}{3} \times \text{LTV}$.
* **Format:** Currency (`₹#,##0`).

### 2.2 Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)
* **Definition:** Ratio of gross revenue generated by paid marketing channels to the total ad expenditure invested.
* **Formula:**  
  $$\text{ROAS} = \frac{\text{Ad-Attributed Revenue}}{\text{Total Marketing Ad Spend}}$$
* **Target / Benchmark:** $> 2.50\text{x}$ blended ($> 3.0\text{x}$ on search, $> 1.8\text{x}$ on paid social).
* **Format:** Decimal ratio (`0.00x`).

### 2.3 Click-Through Rate (CTR)
* **Definition:** Percentage of impressions that result in an ad click to the NutriBox storefront.
* **Formula:**  
  $$\text{CTR} = \left(\frac{\text{Clicks}}{\text{Impressions}}\right) \times 100$$
* **Data Lineage:** `marketing.csv` (`clicks`, `impressions`).
* **Format:** Percentage (`0.00%`).

### 2.4 Cost Per Click (CPC)
* **Definition:** Average price paid per prospective customer visiting the NutriBox platform via paid channels.
* **Formula:**  
  $$\text{CPC} = \frac{\text{Total Marketing Spend}}{\text{Total Clicks}}$$
* **Data Lineage:** `marketing.csv` (`spend`, `clicks`).
* **Format:** Currency (`₹0.00`).

### 2.5 Conversion Rate (CVR %)
* **Definition:** The proportion of storefront clicks that convert into an active subscription or first box order.
* **Formula:**  
  $$\text{CVR} = \left(\frac{\text{Conversions}}{\text{Clicks}}\right) \times 100$$
* **Data Lineage:** `marketing.csv` (`conversions`, `clicks`).
* **Target / Benchmark:** $> 3.00\%$.
* **Format:** Percentage (`0.00%`).

## 3. Financial Health & Runway KPIs

### 3.1 Gross Margin %
* **Definition:** The percentage of revenue remaining after accounting for direct ingredient, packaging, and manufacturing costs (COGS).
* **Formula:**  
  $$\text{Gross Margin \%} = \left(\frac{\text{Delivered Revenue} - \text{COGS}}{\text{Delivered Revenue}}\right) \times 100$$
* **Data Lineage:** `orders.csv` / `finance.csv` (`revenue`, `cogs`).
* **Target / Benchmark:** $45\% - 55\%$ for premium healthy snacking.
* **Format:** Percentage (`0.00%`).

### 3.2 Monthly Net Burn Rate
* **Definition:** Net cash deficit burned each month to fund operations, marketing, and team salaries exceeding gross profit.
* **Formula:**  
  $$\text{Net Burn} = (\text{COGS} + \text{OpEx} + \text{Salaries} + \text{Marketing Spend}) - \text{Delivered Revenue}$$
* **Data Lineage:** `finance.csv` (`cogs`, `opex`, `salaries`, `marketing_spend`, `revenue`).
* **Format:** Currency (`₹#,##0 / month`).

### 3.3 Cash Runway (Months)
* **Definition:** Number of operating months remaining before cash balance reaches zero at the current trailing net burn rate.
* **Formula:**  
  $$\text{Runway (Months)} = \frac{\text{Current Cash Balance}}{\text{Average Trailing 3-Month Net Burn Rate}}$$
* **Data Lineage:** `finance.csv` (`cash_balance`, `net_profit`).
* **Target / Benchmark:** Minimum 6.0 months; target 8.0 - 12.0 months.
* **Format:** Decimal (`0.0 months`).

### 3.4 Net Profit / (Loss)
* **Definition:** Bottom-line monthly or weekly EBITDA before taxes.
* **Formula:**  
  $$\text{Net Profit} = \text{Revenue} - \text{COGS} - \text{OpEx} - \text{Salaries} - \text{Marketing Spend}$$
* **Data Lineage:** `finance.csv` (`net_profit`).
* **Format:** Currency (`₹#,##0`).

## 4. Operations & Customer Experience KPIs

### 4.1 Pending Orders
* **Definition:** Orders awaiting fulfillment and courier dispatch (typically concentrated in the most recent 3-4 days).
* **Formula:**  
  $$\text{Pending Orders} = \text{COUNT}(\text{order\_id}) \quad \text{where } \text{status} = \text{'Pending'}$$
* **Data Lineage:** `orders.csv` (`status`).
* **Format:** Integer (`#,##0`).

### 4.2 Refund Rate %
* **Definition:** The percentage of orders placed during the period that resulted in customer refunds due to quality issues, transit damage, or cancellation.
* **Formula:**  
  $$\text{Refund Rate \%} = \left(\frac{\text{Count of Refunded Orders}}{\text{Total Orders Placed}}\right) \times 100$$
* **Data Lineage:** `orders.csv` (`refund_flag`, `order_id`).
* **Normal Threshold:** $< 2.5\%$. Alert trigger: $> 5.0\%$.
* **Format:** Percentage (`0.00%`).

### 4.3 Average Delivery Time (Days)
* **Definition:** The mean duration in calendar days from order placement to doorstep delivery across India.
* **Formula:**  
  $$\text{Avg Delivery Time} = \frac{\sum \text{delivery\_days}}{\text{Delivered Order Count}}$$
* **Data Lineage:** `orders.csv` (`delivery_days`).
* **Target:** $< 4.0\text{ days}$ nationwide.
* **Format:** Decimal (`0.00 days`).

### 4.4 Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT)
* **Definition:** Mean rating given by customers regarding snack quality and delivery experience on a 1-5 scale.
* **Formula:**  
  $$\text{CSAT} = \frac{\sum \text{csat\_score}}{\text{Count of Feedback Responses}}$$
* **Data Lineage:** `feedback.csv` (`csat_score`).
* **Target / Benchmark:** $\ge 4.20 / 5.00$.
* **Format:** Decimal (`0.00 / 5.0`).

### 4.5 Net Promoter Score (NPS)
* **Definition:** Standard loyalty index measuring customer likelihood to recommend NutriBox to peers.
  - Promoters: Score 9 or 10
  - Passives: Score 7 or 8
  - Detractors: Score 0 to 6
* **Formula:**  
  $$\text{NPS} = \left(\frac{\text{Promoters} - \text{Detractors}}{\text{Total NPS Respondents}}\right) \times 100$$
* **Data Lineage:** `feedback.csv` (`nps_score`).
* **Target / Benchmark:** $> +50$ (Excellent for consumer packaged goods).
* **Format:** Index score (`-100 to +100`).

## 5. Strategic Unit Economics & Growth KPIs

### 5.1 Week-over-Week (WoW) Growth %
* **Definition:** Percentage expansion or contraction in a metric relative to the immediate preceding week ($W_{t-1}$).
* **Formula:**  
  $$\text{WoW \%} = \left(\frac{M_t - M_{t-1}}{M_{t-1}}\right) \times 100$$
* **Format:** Percentage (`+0.00% / -0.00%`).

### 5.2 Month-over-Month (MoM) Growth %
* **Definition:** Expansion in monthly delivered revenue compared to previous calendar month.
* **Formula:**  
  $$\text{MoM \%} = \left(\frac{\text{Rev}_m - \text{Rev}_{m-1}}{\text{Rev}_{m-1}}\right) \times 100$$
* **Format:** Percentage (`+0.00% / -0.00%`).

### 5.3 Monthly Churn Rate %
* **Definition:** The percentage of active subscribers who terminate their subscription within the month (or weekly equivalent normalized by 4.333).
* **Formula:**  
  $$\text{Weekly Churn \%} = \left(\frac{\text{Cancellations in Week}}{\text{Active Subscribers at Week Start}}\right) \times 100$$
  $$\text{Monthly Churn \%} \approx \text{Weekly Churn \%} \times 4.333$$
* **Target / Benchmark:** $4.0\% - 6.0\%$ monthly.
* **Format:** Percentage (`0.00%`).

### 5.4 Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)
* **Definition:** The average monthly subscription monetization generated per active subscriber.
* **Formula:**  
  $$\text{ARPU} = \frac{\text{Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)}}{\text{Active Customers}}$$
* **Format:** Currency (`₹#,##0`).

### 5.5 Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
* **Definition:** The net gross profit contribution generated by a customer across their expected tenure with NutriBox.
* **Formula:**  
  $$\text{LTV} = \frac{\text{ARPU} \times \text{Gross Margin \%}}{\text{Monthly Churn Rate}}$$
* **Format:** Currency (`₹#,##0`).

### 5.6 LTV to CAC Ratio (LTV:CAC)
* **Definition:** The fundamental indicator of unit economic sustainability and acquisition capital efficiency.
* **Formula:**  
  $$\text{LTV:CAC} = \frac{\text{LTV}}{\text{CAC}}$$
* **Target / Benchmark:** $> 3.0\text{x}$ (healthy business); $< 1.5\text{x}$ indicates unsustainable acquisition costs.
* **Format:** Decimal ratio (`0.00x`).
